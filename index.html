01—04

The central scientific tension

Dynamic genome activity. Stable cellular identity.

Cells share essentially the same genome, yet establish diverse and persistent identities while transcription, RNA metabolism and chromatin organization continually fluctuate across space and time.

Molecular reality

Sparse · Stochastic · Dynamic

Genome activity is transient and heterogeneous within individual cells.

→
Biological outcome

Diverse · Reproducible · Stable

Cellular states persist across time and populations.

Biological information flow

Four operations transform fluctuation into identity.

The laboratory connects genome organization, the RNA–RBP meshwork, transcriptional surveillance and probabilistic cell-state dynamics in one continuous framework.

Research programs

Four questions across one information system.

Explore all research ↗
01 Organization Genome Organization How does genome sequence encode nuclear organization? We investigate how genome sequences, repetitive elements and their RNA products establish nuclear architecture, spatial positioning and genome regulation. Explore program ↗ 02 Processing RNA Networks How does RNA organize the nuclear environment? We study how nascent RNA, RNA-binding proteins and processing factors form an RNA–RBP meshwork that coordinates genome function. Explore program ↗ 03 Selection Transcriptional Surveillance How does productive information emerge from pervasive transcription? We examine how RNA Polymerase II, RNA processing and degradation pathways support productive elongation, RNA maturation and polymerase recycling. Explore program ↗ 04 Stabilization Cell Fate Dynamics How do probabilistic genome activities generate stable cell identity? We measure and model how sparse, stochastic genome activity is coordinated across individual cells and stabilized across populations. Explore program ↗
